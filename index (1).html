The Yash Taco Shop

A three-page website for a family-owned taco shop: a home page with story and menu, a store hours page, and a contact page with a working form. Written in HTML5 with a shared stylesheet.

index.html

  • Header with navigation, hero figure and caption
  • “About” article with a collapsible Taco Trivia section
  • Menu table: carne asada, pollo and carnitas at $3.50

What this project shows

Semantic HTML5

header, nav, main, article, aside, figure, address, time and abbr used for meaning, not just layout.

Accessible forms

Every input has a linked label, and the fields use built-in browser validation.

Content structure

Tables, definition lists and disclosure widgets chosen to fit the type of content.

Multi-page navigation

Consistent navigation and in-page anchors across all three pages.
